## The decision

## Why it is an EXPERIMENT and not a cure

**The degeneracy has now recurred twice, on two different models** — `stepfun/step-5-preview:free`, then
`deepseek-v4.1-flash`. **That is why the model is a contributor and not the cause**, and why the Chair is
not claiming context length either. Context length is a **hypothesis**, and this is the cheapest way to
test it.

**The observation, named in advance so either outcome is a result:**

- **Does the degeneracy recur within the next N turns of a fresh session?**
- **If it does not recur:** context length is **supported as a contributor.** Still not proven — one
  clean run is not a series, and that rule has been broken by both of us today.
- **If it recurs:** context length is **not** the driver, **the session variable is eliminated**, and the
  search moves on with one candidate closed.
- **Either outcome is information.** The alternative — continuing to receive recurrence reports from a
  context nobody has varied — produces none.

**Framed as a test deliberately:** a fix sold as a cause is the exact error this hour was spent
correcting, and the Chair made it first.

## The rejected alternative, stated plainly

**Compressing the existing session.** It preserves the thread's continuity, **but it leaves the variable
half-changed** — a partially compressed context may or may not behave like a fresh one, so the
observation would be **uninterpretable**. It buys continuity at the price of the answer. **If continuity
matters more than the measurement on some future occasion, that is a legitimate trade** — it just isn't
this one, because the whole point here is to learn which variable is doing the work.

- **It does not say the loop is harmless.** It corrupted a whole delivery once and deliberation twice.
  **The severity changed; the presence did not.**
- **It does not close the inbound-leg line.** A falsified *explanation* is not a falsified
  *observation*: the store tables are unused install-wide (measured across three profiles), **but the
  ticket-queue counts came from the mailbox surface and still stand unmeasured.**
