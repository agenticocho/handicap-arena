# WHAT A SEED CONTROLS, measured from snake/snake.html rather than assumed: the board is 25x25 cells, the
# snake starts at cell (10,10) moving right, and the first apple is fixed at cell (20,20) BY THE FILE.
# Nothing about the geometry is random. The only randomness in the game is `getRandomInt`, called from
# exactly two places -- when an apple is EATEN and when the board RESETS after a self-collision -- and
# the wrapper replaces `Math.random` with a seeded PRNG before the page's script runs. So the seed fixes
# the apple respawn sequence, and (seed, move list) fixes the whole episode.
