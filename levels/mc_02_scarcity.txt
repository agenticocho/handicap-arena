# SEVEN THREATS, FOUR ROUNDS. It is IMPOSSIBLE to stop everything, and that is deliberate: the event
# measures the CHOICE, not the reflexes. Four cities and seven incoming means the entrant must decide
# which ground to lose.
#
# AND THE DECOYS ARE THE INSTRUMENT. Three threats (X, Y, Z) impact EMPTY GROUND at 7, 8 and 9. Nine is
# outside every silo's reach, so Z cannot be engaged by anyone even if the entrant wants to. A model that
# fires at everything runs out of ammo and loses cities it could have saved; a model that reads the impact
# coordinates spends only on what matters and finishes with more cities alive.
#
# The ground line is 0..9. Cities sit at 2, 3, 5 and 6.
#   P -> 2  city      Q -> 3  city      R -> 5  city      S -> 6  city      T -> 4  city
#   X -> 7  EMPTY     Y -> 8  EMPTY     Z -> 9  EMPTY and OUT OF RANGE
#
# Silo 1 reaches 0..4, silo 2 reaches 5..9. So there is no silo that can cover the whole board, which is
# what forces the routing decision on top of the ammo decision.
