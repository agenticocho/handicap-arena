## 7. Findings this window produced (in order of how much they change)

1. **The snake certification is not on the trunk.** `cabinet/snake @ 149d0d0` holds the certified driver
   (`snake_run.py` 42,349 B, `c2aabbaf…`, writes `score.revisits`, CPU-only); the trunk holds a divergent
   sitting-2 revision (44,256 B, `a4fc47ce…`) that accepts the GPU flags and omits the certified field.
   This is the same class as the sokoban card's five copies at five revisions, and it is the reason pass 1
   of snake is in the record as INSTRUMENT-FAILURE rather than as a result.
2. **A published sokoban solve did not survive re-execution.** Sitting 2 recorded bonsai-9b as
   `solved=True` on level 01; under the replay verifier that cell is REFUSED (the plan runs 40 tokens and
   none of them is the solving move). The new row says so; the old row is preserved with its digest.
3. **"CPU-only" does not mean the card is untouched.** An MC CPU block (`-ngl 0`) allocates a CUDA context
   of **+364 MiB** (1779 → 2143 while it ran). One snake pass-2 block sampled its baseline during that
   window and therefore reads `delta -364`; the disagreement is mine, not the cabinet's, and it is the one
   block in the window that fails the ±64 MiB release rule.
4. **chess's `illegal-move` reason carries neither the ply nor the FEN**, and I misread it as the
   sitting-2 defect before counting the rows: the artifact's own control shows `e2e4` legal at startpos
   and row 0 shows it played, while the termination's `why` quotes `e2e4` as illegal — at **ply 1**, where
   Black repeated it. A reason string that cannot name its own position produced a false alarm in the
   reviewer this card was written for.
5. **MC's "17/17" is invocation-dependent**: bare 12/12, `--denominator` 13/13,
   `--denominator --mutations` 17/17. The audit document does name the guard line that produces 17; a
   reader who runs it bare is looking at a different count of the same thing.
6. **The card's baseline instruction was right**: the card sat at 1789 MiB at the open, 1779 mid-window
   and 1795 later. Assuming 1,731 would have failed the release check on every block or hidden a real one.
